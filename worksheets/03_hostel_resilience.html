Evidence Base & Credits

Designed & Developed by: B. Bhuvan, School Counsellor, M.Sc. Psychology, DCGC, NCERT
Institutional Context: Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS), G. Madugula
Measurement Note: Anonymized batch-level tallies only; no student names or identifiers are stored.

This strength-based residential school workshop avoids public emotional escalation and instead builds cognitive reappraisal, daily routine mastery, peer belonging, and concrete anti-distraction implementation plans grounded in peer-reviewed psychology:

  • 1. Dual-Process Adjustment & Preventive Homesickness Coping: Thurber, C. A., & Walton, E. A. (2007). Preventing and treating homesickness. Pediatrics, 119(1), 192–201; and Thurber, C. A., & Walton, E. A. (2012). Homesickness and adjustment in university students. Journal of American College Health, 60(5), 415–419.
  • 2. Ordinary Magic Resilience Systems (Routine, Belonging & Mastery): Masten, A. S. (2001). Ordinary magic: Resilience processes in development. American Psychologist, 56(3), 227–238; and Masten, A. S. (2014). Ordinary Magic: Resilience in Development. Guilford Press.
  • 3. Cognitive Appraisal & Problem-Focused Coping: Lazarus, R. S., & Folkman, S. (1984). Stress, Appraisal, and Coping. Springer Publishing. Operationalizes threat vs. challenge appraisal and active coping over passive rumination.
  • 4. Implementation Intentions (“If–Then” Anti-Distraction Plans): Gollwitzer, P. M. (1999). Implementation intentions: Strong effects of simple plans. American Psychologist, 54(7), 493–503.
