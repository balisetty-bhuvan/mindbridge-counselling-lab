      The NO – GO – TELL Action Simulator

      Protective Path Practised: 0 / 3 Scenarios
      1. NO
      Firm voice & clear boundary — “Stop! I don’t like this.” No guilt, no apology needed.
      2. GO
      Move immediately to a safe, public place with other people or trusted adults.
      3. TELL
      Report to a trusted adult (Counsellor, Warden, Principal, Parent) or call 1098/112. If the first adult doesn’t listen, KEEP TELLING until someone acts!

          References & Legal Basis

          Created & Facilitated By B. Bhuvan, School Counsellor, M.Sc. Psychology, DCGC, NCERT
          • 1. The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 (as amended in 2019) & POCSO Rules, 2020: Sections 3–15 (contact & non-contact offences including Section 11 sexual harassment), Section 19 (Mandatory Reporting), Section 21 (accountability for failure to report), Section 23 (strict prohibition on disclosing child’s identity), Sections 33 & 37 (child-friendly Special Court procedures and in-camera trials).
          • 2. National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR): User Handbook on the POCSO Act & POCSO e-Box Confidential Online Complaint Mechanism (ncpcr.gov.in).
          • 3. Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD), Government of India: Mission Vatsalya 24×7 Toll-Free Child Helpline 1098 (integrated with National Emergency Response Support System 112) and National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (1930 / cybercrime.gov.in).
          • 4. NCERT & UNICEF India: Adolescent Life Skills & Personal Safety Education Guidelines (globally validated NO–GO–TELL Protective Action Protocol).
          • 5. Protective Behaviours Framework: Two Core Themes — “We all have the right to feel safe all the time” and “We can talk with someone about anything, even if it feels awful or small.”
          • 6. Author & Facilitator: B. Bhuvan, School Counsellor, M.Sc. Psychology, DCGC, NCERT. Designed for preventive psychoeducation, classroom empowerment, and anonymized group outcome evaluation.
